Users must only upload videos they own or are authorized to edit. Uploaded media may be processed by our video workers and AI providers to perform transcription, clip selection and rendering.

Payments are handled by our payment provider. ClipForge does not store full card details.

YouTube

If you connect YouTube, ClipForge uses the permissions you authorize to support channel and video publishing features.
